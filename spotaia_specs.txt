**[SPOTAIA APPLICATION SPECIFICATIONS]**

### 1. General Overview & Theme
- **App Name:** Spotaia
- **Niche:** Booking sports venues (Football, Padel) and entertainment lounges (PlayStation, etc.), plus a player-matching community.
- **Theme:** Dark Neon. Backgrounds (`#121212`, `#0A0A0A`), Primary Color Neon Blue (`#00E5FF`), Secondary Color Neon Orange (`#FF9100`).
- **Tech Stack:** 
  - **Frontend:** Flutter Web/Mobile, Riverpod (State Management), GoRouter (Navigation), Dio (HTTP), flutter_animate (Animations).
  - **Backend:** Node.js, Express, TypeScript.
  - **Database & ORM:** PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM.
  - **Auth:** JWT (JSON Web Tokens).

### 2. Backend Architecture & Database Schema
- **Concurrency Handling:** Implemented strict Pessimistic Row-Level Locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` via Prisma `$executeRawUnsafe`) in the `POST /api/bookings` endpoint to completely eliminate race conditions and double-bookings under extreme concurrent load.
- **Models:**
  - `User`: `id`, `phone` (unique), `name`, `passwordHash`, `role` (PLAYER, OWNER, ADMIN), `profilePic`, `points` (Gamification), `level` (e.g., BRONZE, SILVER, GOLD), `matchesPlayed`.
  - `Pitch` (Venues): `id`, `ownerId`, `category` (Football, Padel, PlayStation, etc.), `name`, `description`, `location`, `openTime`, `closeTime`, `surface`, `indoor` (boolean), `pricePerHour`, `images` (JSON array), `rating`, `totalReviews`.
  - `Booking`: `id`, `pitchId`, `userId`, `startTime`, `endTime`, `status`, `price`.
  - `Review`: `id`, `pitchId`, `userId`, `rating`, `comment`.
  - `MatchRequest`: `id`, `creatorId`, `title`, `description`, `matchTime`, `missingSpots`, `costPerSpot`, `status`.

### 3. Frontend Features & Screens

#### A. Authentication (`AuthScreen`)
- Login and Registration forms.
- Users choose their role upon registration: "لاعب" (Player) or "صاحب منشأة" (Owner).
- Beautiful Spotaia Logo with scaling animation (`BoxFit.contain` wrapped in `Center` to prevent stretch).

#### B. Main Navigation (`HomeScreen`)
- Uses `NavigationBar` with 4 Tabs. Icons have `flutter_animate` scale and pop effects on selection.
- **Tab 1: Venues (الملاعب المتاحة):**
  - Horizontal scrollable `ChoiceChip` for categories (All, Football, Padel, PlayStation).
  - Venues list dynamically filters based on category selection.
  - Venue cards show images, location, price, and **Virtual Points** earned per hour (calculated as `pricePerHour * 0.5`). Cards enter the screen with staggered Fade and SlideX animations.
- **Tab 2: Community (مجتمع اللاعبين):**
  - Fetches from `/api/match-requests`. Shows posts from players looking for others to join their match and split the cost.
- **Tab 3: Leaderboard (الترتيب):**
  - Fetches top players sorted by `points` descending. 
  - Top 3 players have special UI treatments (Gold 🥇, Silver 🥈, Bronze 🥉 highlighting).
- **Tab 4: Profile (حسابي):**
  - Displays User Info, Role, Avatar.
  - **Edit Profile:** Opens a dialog to update Name, Phone, and Profile Picture (PUT `/api/users/me`).
  - **My Bookings:** Displays a list of the user's personal bookings fetched from the database, rendered with staggered animations.

#### C. Venue Details (`PitchDetailsScreen`)
- Displays venue details, amenities, and available time slots.
- User can select a Date and Start/End times to calculate the total price and book.
- **Review System Logic:** The "Add Review" button ONLY appears if the user has a confirmed booking for this specific venue AND has not reviewed it before.

#### D. Owner Dashboard (`OwnerDashboardScreen` & `ManagePitchScreen`)
- Owners see their total revenue, total bookings, and a list of their owned venues.
- Owners can add/edit venues, defining name, category (Dropdown), price, times, surface type, and upload image links.

### 4. Current UX/UI Transitions
- **Page Routing:** GoRouter uses `FadeTransition` for pushing screens (e.g., from Home to Pitch Details).
- **Tab Switching:** Uses `AnimatedSwitcher` wrapping a `SlideTransition` and `FadeTransition` (300ms) for ultra-smooth tab switching.
- **Micro-interactions:** Buttons and Navigation Bar items use `flutter_animate` (scale/fade/slide) when rendered or interacted with. Global `splashColor` is Neon Blue with 30% opacity for touch ripples.
